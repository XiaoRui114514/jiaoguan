## Changes For This Game

Recordings were resampled to 44.1 kHz, balanced in volume, and converted to MP3
(music and ambience) or PCM WAV (short effects). Loop boundaries were blended;
short effects received small fades. Correct Bell was rearranged into a softer
four-note school chime with pitch and timing changes. No voices were added.

Source licenses were checked on 2026-10-02.
